About Rebecca Sugar

Rebecca Sugar is an American animator, storyboard artist, screenwriter, producer, director, and musician. Rebecca Sugar is the first non-binary person to produce an animated series for Cartoon Network. They are nonbinary, genderqueer and bisexual. Rebecca uses she/they pronouns.

Rebecca Sugar has worked as a storyboard artist and joined the team to work on "Adventure Time" during it's first season. She has put a lot of her own life experiences into the character Marceline. They left during the fifth season to focus on the production of "Steven Universe". Sugar also worked on anti racism PSAs featuring Steven Universe Characters.

Acolades

Rebecca's work on Adventure Time gained a Primetime Emmy Award for Short-format Animation nominations. The show also earned several Annie Award nominations.

She has received, among others, the 2018 Peabody Award for Children's & Youth Programming and the 2019 GLAAD Media Award for Outstanding Kids & Family Program

Rebecca was also featured in Forbes 30 under 30 for writing "many of the best epsiodes of Adventure Time"

"In 2025, a South American flower of the genus Commelina was named Commelina sugariae in honour of Sugar's artistic achievements. The same authors named another species C. almandina, a name referring to the garnet colour of the "gem-like" fruits, in honour of the character Garnet from Steven Universe, "because it brings much-needed attention to BIPOC and LGBTQIA+ researchers in STEM."
