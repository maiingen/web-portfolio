Why is Rebecca Sugar my favouritie musician?

I was first introduced to them through her animated series "Steven Universe". I fell in love with the show. It was the first time I was seeing queerness in a positive light on a kids show. I also started falling in love with the music throughout the show. They broke a lot of social norms in both their music and the shows they've been apart of. Rebecca Sugar also wrote some music for "Adventure Time"
