Two-Spirit Tales

Atmosphere

Two-Spirit Tales is a safe space that is operated and owned by Indigenous, Queer, and Two-Spirit folks. We have a cozy atmosphere with natural lighting. We have a sensory safe room. We also have tons of different types of seating available. We have SAD lamps for light therapy. Two-Spirit Tales is fully accessible.

Location

We are located in Hamilton Ontario

Hours

We are open Monday-Sunday 11am-11pm
